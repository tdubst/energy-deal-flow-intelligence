# ERCOT Battery Tell Pilot

## Offer

Energy Deal Flow Intelligence helps infrastructure investors, data center site-selection teams, and land acquisition groups find pre-headline power and land opportunities before they become obvious public news.

The first paid product is a focused ERCOT Battery Tell pilot: a map-first intelligence package that screens BESS projects with strong interconnection signals, then ranks nearby parcels by transmission proximity, acreage, flood/buildability, and estimated land-spread upside.

## Target Customers

- Data center developers and site-selection teams
- Infrastructure and private equity investors
- Energy developers pursuing BESS, renewables, gas, or hybrid projects
- Land assemblers and real estate developers near power-constrained growth corridors
- Power/data-center advisory firms and brokers

## Pilot Deliverables

- Interactive ERCOT BESS opportunity map
- 20-50 ranked BESS / storage project opportunities
- Parcel candidate screen around priority POIs
- HIFLD 345kV transmission proximity scoring
- FEMA flood/buildability screen
- Estimated screening upside: low/base/high
- Exportable ranked projects and parcels CSVs
- 5 narrative deal pages for top opportunities
- 30-45 minute client briefing

## Pricing

- Lite corridor screen: $5,000
- ERCOT-wide standard pilot: $12,500
- Premium pilot with custom diligence and briefing: $25,000

## Example Output

For Avalon BESS / Fort Bend, the MVP currently ranks real FBCAD parcel candidates by score, buildable acreage, distance to 345kV transmission, flood risk, and estimated screening upside.

The output should be positioned as a deal-screening tool, not an appraisal, engineering study, or investment recommendation.
