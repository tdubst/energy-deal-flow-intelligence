# Infrastructure Intelligence

Product goal: Identify early infrastructure signals before they become obvious news.

Priority signals:
- Interconnection queue filings and agreement execution dates.
- BESS / renewable project size, voltage level, and POI.
- Transmission/substation proximity.
- Parcel acreage, ownership fragmentation, and buildability.
- Flood constraints and local development risk.
- Financing events, land purchases, MOUs, annexations, and local news.

Public data stack:
- ERCOT interconnection queue.
- HIFLD transmission lines.
- FEMA flood data.
- County parcel data.
- EIA / FERC datasets.
- Local news and press releases.
