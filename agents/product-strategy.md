# Product Strategy

Long-term moat:
- Infrastructure signal synthesis.
- Corridor intelligence.
- Diligence acceleration.
- Opportunity ranking.
- Narrative generation.
- Workflow integration.

Initial customer targets:
- Data center site-selection teams.
- Infrastructure and private equity investors.
- Energy developers.
- Land assemblers.
- Power and data-center advisory firms.

Positioning: pre-headline deal-screening intelligence, not appraisal, engineering, or investment advice.
